Dungeon Dogs merc GM cheat sheet
================================
Syntax checked against MercCommand.cs / Mercs.TryParseTier / Mercs.Kinds.
Tiers above basic need GM level 1. Body level stays at your level (cap 220); tiers twink gear only.
Profession keys (aliases in parentheses): enforcer (enf), soldier (sol), keeper (keep),
doctor (doc), adventurer (adv), agent, bureaucrat (crat), engineer (engi, eng), fixer (fix),
martialartist (ma, martial), metaphysicist (mp, meta), nanotechnician (nt, nano), shade, trader.
Tier words: basic|normal, elite|e, champion|champ|c, twink|t|uber|u|super|s
  (uber and super are accepted as aliases for twink)
